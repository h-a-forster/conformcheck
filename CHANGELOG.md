### Added

- Project scaffold: uv/hatchling package, lockfile, CI (pull requests and manual runs).
- `docs/DESIGN.md`: oracle, exact laws, study design, milestones and pre-registered
  hypotheses.
- `conformcheck.laws`: exact split-conformal order statistic, marginal coverage and
  bounds, Beta and BetaBinomial laws, discrete-score (tied) laws, asymmetric CQR
  coverage, jackknife+ and CV+ bounds.
- Fixed-function scikit-learn models, the adapter contract, import stubs for TorchCP's
  unused heavy dependencies, a minimal CLI and the pytest plugin skeleton.
